Sour Soup

Pork Sinigang

Sinigang na Baboy

⏱ 60 min 🍚 Serves 4–6

Pork Sinigang is a classic Filipino sour soup that balances savory, tangy, and rich flavors. The heart of the dish is a souring agent — traditionally fresh, unripe tamarind (sampalok) or a tamarind soup mix — which creates a sharp, mouth-watering broth. Marbled pork, like belly (liempo), spare ribs, or shoulder, simmers with onions, tomatoes, and fish sauce until tender, rendering its fat into the broth for a deeply satisfying richness.

What makes Sinigang remarkable is its colorful medley of fresh vegetables that soak up the tangy soup: long green chilies for gentle warmth, plus radish, string beans, eggplant, and okra for varied texture, finished with a handful of water spinach (kangkong) right before serving. Best enjoyed piping hot over a mountain of steamed rice, with fish sauce and crushed chili on the side.

Ingredients

The Meat & Broth Base

  • 1 kg (2.2 lbs) pork — bone-in cuts like spare ribs, chops, or marbled belly (liempo), cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 1.5–2 liters water — or rice washing, for a thicker, starchier broth
  • 2 medium red onions — quartered
  • 3 medium tomatoes — quartered
  • 3–4 tbsp fish sauce (patis) — adjust to taste

The Souring Agent

  • 1 pack (40g) tamarind soup base mix — standard size; or 10–15 pieces fresh green tamarind, boiled and mashed for juice

Vegetables & Aromatics

  • 1 medium daikon radish (labanos) — peeled, sliced into 1/2-inch rounds
  • 1 bunch string beans (sitaw) — cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 long eggplant (talong) — sliced diagonally, 1/2-inch thick
  • 6–8 pieces okra — tops trimmed, left whole
  • 2–3 long green chilies (siling haba) — left whole for aroma and mild warmth
  • 1 bunch water spinach (kangkong) — leaves and tender stems separated from tough stalks
  • Salt & black pepper — to taste

Method

Prepping & Searing

10 min
  1. (Optional but recommended) Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pot over medium-high heat and sear the pork for 3–5 minutes until light brown on all sides, to lock in the juices.
  2. Add the quartered onions and tomatoes. Sauté with the pork for 2 minutes until softened and fragrant.

Simmering the Pork

30–45 min
  1. Pour in 1.5–2 liters of water (or rice washing) and bring to a rolling boil.
  2. Reduce heat to medium-low and skim off any gray foam or impurities to keep the broth clear.
  3. Season with fish sauce and a pinch of black pepper. Cover and simmer 30–45 minutes, until the pork is fork-tender.

Souring & Hard Vegetables

10 min
  1. Stir in the tamarind soup mix until fully dissolved. Taste and adjust with more fish sauce or salt if needed.
  2. Add the sliced radish and whole green chilies. Cover and simmer for 5 minutes.

Finishing & Greens

5 min
  1. Add the string beans, okra, and eggplant. Push down into the broth, cover, and cook 3–4 minutes until tender but still vibrant.
  2. Add the water spinach stems and leaves. Turn off the heat, cover, and let the residual heat wilt the greens for 1–2 minutes.

To serve: ladle piping hot into deep bowls with warm rice, alongside a small saucer of fish sauce and crushed chili for dipping.
